## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a luxury, high-fashion editorial presence fused with sharp digital precision. Engineered for high-profile creative directors, designers, and visual artists, it balances stark minimalism with dramatic theatricality. The aesthetic relies on an uncompromising deep matte black environment punctuated by aggressive crimson accents and razor-thin hairlines.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Dramatic Minimalist Hierarchy:** Giant condensed display titles establish scale and architectural posture, contrasting with refined micro-labels and airy tracking.
- **Architectural Framing:** Clean grid networks defined by 1px translucent hairline borders replace soft, drop-shadow elevations.
- **Atmospheric Tension:** Deep crimson tones provide controlled heat against cold, void-black surfaces, accented by delicate four-point sparkles (`✦`) and technical metadata.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict dark mode, establishing extreme contrast between absolute matte darks and vivid chromatic reds. 

### Color Strategy
- **Master Canvas (`#0A0A0A`):** The foundational substrate across all views, absorbing light and eliminating visual distraction.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#121212`, `#161616`):** Used sparingly for structural cards, project preview containers, and media tiles to construct planar elevation without diffuse drop shadows.
- **Crimson Accents (`#D92323`, `#8A1111`):** The primary color acts as a high-visibility laser beacon—reserved strictly for index numbers, active state borders, inline quotation marks, and key interactive highlights.
- **Hairlines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`):** Architectural grid delineators that segment content horizontally and vertically.

## Typography

The typography system relies on a structural juxtaposition: condensed, unyielding display headings set in **Anton**, contrasted with the geometric, high-legibility precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

### Typographic Rules
- **Headline Capitalization:** Headings using Anton and section action anchors are set in full uppercase to reinforce industrial geometry.
- **Numerics & Indices:** All quantitative counters, step progression numbers (`01`, `02`), and large metrics utilize Anton.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Expressive italic nuances (such as greetings or personal sign-offs) layer warm humanist cues over the rigid architectural body sans.
- **Micro Typography:** Category labels, status tags, and eyebrow indicators are small (`10px`–`11px`), bold, and tracked widely (`0.10em` to `0.16em`) to preserve legibility against deep dark surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model operates as an editorial, multi-column bento system contained within a max width of `1320px` with fluid outer canvas margins (`5vw`).

### Grid Strategy
- **Projects Grid:** 3 equal columns (`1fr 1fr 1fr`) separated by `28px` (`1.75rem`) gutters on desktop, collapsing to a single-column stack below `900px`.
- **Credentials Bento Grid:** An asymmetric 3-column distribution (`1.15fr 1fr 0.85fr`) separated by vertical hairline borders, reflowing into `1fr 1fr` below `1024px` and a single column below `760px`.
- **Contact & Action Grid:** 3-column split (`1fr 1.1fr 1.1fr`) collapsing to a single column below `960px`.

### Spacing Rhythms
- Base layout increments strictly adhere to 4px and 8px modules.
- Major vertical sections are spaced with `space-3xl` (`80px`) padding, demarcated with edge-to-edge `1px` translucent hairlines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through structural layering, ambient radial luminescence, and hairline containment rather than traditional skeuomorphic drop shadows.

### Elevation Principles
- **Matte Base:** The global view rests on `#0A0A0A`. Surface elevation is signified by low-luminance steps to `#121212` and `#161616`.
- **Atmospheric Radial Glow:** Controlled, deep ambient lighting (e.g., `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(160, 20, 20, 0.22) 0%, transparent 60%)`) blooms behind focal subjects to separate foreground content from the background plane.
- **Hairline Bounding:** Surfaces are outlined with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, creating clean boundaries that eliminate the need for cast shadows.
- **Z-Index System:**
  - `z-index: 1`: Giant background display lettering
  - `z-index: 3`: Subject assets and imagery
  - `z-index: 10`: Structural text cards, floating technical badges, and metrics
  - `z-index: 20`: Navigation bar and floating overlays

## Shapes

The design language favors architectural, sharp geometry. Structural foundations (cards, image frames, and content partitions) feature rigid `0px` to `0.25rem` (`4px`) radii, providing a sharp editorial framework.

### Corner Radius Usage
- **Cards, Frames, and Cells:** `roundedness: 1` (`4px`), maintaining clean silhouettes and sharp transitions.
- **Tag Pills and Interactive Micro-badges:** Retain slight softening (`rounded-sm` or `rounded-md`) to ensure tactile differentiation from structural grid boxes.
- **Indicator Rings and Action Buttons:** Full circle (`50%` pill shapes) reserved exclusively for navigation nodes, process timeline badges, and contact trigger points.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action Button:** Circular or compact pill container, bordered with `1px solid var(--accent)` (`#D92323`). Background rests as pure dark or deep crimson (`#140808`), transitioning to solid `#D92323` on hover with crisp `#FFFFFF` iconography.
- **Text Links & Action Rows:** Tracked uppercase labels (`11px`, weight 700) accompanied by horizontal arrows (`→`). On `:hover`, the arrow translates right by `4px` with a color shift from `#8E8E93` to `#FFFFFF`.

### Chips & Tag Pills
- **Base State:** Inline flex, padding `8px 14px`. Border is `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.22)`, background transparent, and typography uppercase `10px` Plus Jakarta Sans with `0.10em` tracking in `#E0E0E0`.
- **Hover State:** Background shifts to subtle crimson wash (`rgba(217, 35, 35, 0.12)`), text shifts to `#FFFFFF`, and border highlights to `#D92323`.

### Showcase Project Cards
- **Container:** Rectangular matte frame (`#121212`) wrapped in `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` hairline border.
- **Media Frame:** Fixed ratio thumbnail container in `#161616`. Images scale subtly (`1.04x`) over `0.6s cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` on hover, with the border glowing to `#D92323`.
- **Card Footer:** Segmented by a top hairline divider, pairing an oversized Anton index number (`01`, `02`) in `#D92323` alongside stacked title and category meta.

### Process Timeline
- **Step Markers:** Circular nodes (`38px`) set with `1px solid #D92323` containing clean vector line icons.
- **Connecting Track:** Continuous vertical hairline stroke using `rgba(217, 35, 35, 0.3)`.
- **Step Text:** Anton display index paired with an uppercase step title (`12px`, weight 700) and muted detail copy (`12px`, `#8E8E93`).

### Bento Quote Card
- **Background:** High-drama diagonal gradient fill (`linear-gradient(145deg, #2A0808 0%, #520D0D 100%)`).
- **Elements:** Prominent crimson quotation mark in Anton (`56px`), standard body statement (`20px`), followed by an editorial handwritten signature and uppercase tracking tagline.
